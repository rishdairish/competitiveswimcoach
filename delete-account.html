Delete your SwimPeak account

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Delete it in the app

  1. Open SwimPeak and log in.
  2. Go to Settings and scroll to Delete my account.
  3. Tap Delete my account, enter your password, type DELETE, and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Can't log in?

Email us at swimpeakteam@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my account” and your SwimPeak username. We may ask you to confirm it is really you. We aim to delete the account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, posts, comments and likes
  • Your training, nutrition and AI data
  • Photos and files you uploaded, including your profile picture and banner
  • Your follows, friends, blocks and notifications
  • The messages you sent. The other person keeps their own side of the conversation, with you shown as “Swimmer” and no name or photo.

What may be kept

  • Copies in backups, which are overwritten after a short time.
  • Anything we are legally required to keep.

An account that is suspended or banned cannot be deleted while the suspension is in place. Your Google Play subscription, if you have one, is managed by Google: cancel it in Google Play so you are not charged again.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
