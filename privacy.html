Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy explains what information SwimPeak collects, why we collect it, who can see it, and the choices you have. SwimPeak (“we”, “us”) is operated by The Swim Peak Team. You can contact us at swimpeakteam@gmail.com. By creating an account or using SwimPeak you agree to this policy.

The short version

  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show ads or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • Your email address and date of birth are never shown to other users.
  • You decide who can see your profile and your posts.
  • Things you send to the AI features are processed by third-party AI providers.
  • Direct messages are private between the two people, but they are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and your data.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: email address, password (kept by our sign-in provider as a secure hash, so we cannot read it), first and last name, username, date of birth, country, swimming club (optional), and the details you add to your profile such as bio, profile photo, banner image, main stroke, favourite event and name colour. We use your date of birth to work out your age group and to check that you are old enough to use SwimPeak.
  • Training and wellbeing data: swim sessions and workouts, nutrition and hydration logs, journal entries, recovery information, goals, personal bests and competition results. Some of this is health-related, so we treat it as sensitive: we use it to run the features you use and only show it to others as described in section 3.
  • Photos and videos: your profile and banner pictures, photos in posts, images you send in direct messages, and meal photos, food-label photos or swim videos you upload for AI analysis.
  • Social activity: who you follow and who follows you, your friends, posts, comments, likes, direct messages (text and images), people you block, reports you make or that are made about you, and your notifications.
  • AI interactions: messages you send to the AI Coach and any content you submit to an AI feature, plus a count of how often you use AI features so we can apply usage limits.
  • Premium: if you subscribe, we receive your subscription status and a purchase token from Google Play so we can confirm the purchase. We never see your card details.

Information from your device

  • Your browser or device stores a sign-in session, saved copies of some content so pages load faster, and a few preferences (for example which messages or comments you have hidden, and your Swimmy settings). We do not use tracking cookies.
  • Like most online services, our hosting providers can see technical details such as your IP address and device type when your device connects.
  • The app loads its font from Google Fonts and some code libraries from jsDelivr, so those services also receive your IP address when SwimPeak loads.

2. How we use your information

  • To run SwimPeak: track your training, show your profile, feed, search and leaderboards, and let you follow, message and block other people.
  • To power the AI features, such as workout plans, nutrition and label scanning, technique analysis, journal and race feedback, and AI Coach replies, using what you submit.
  • To send you notifications inside the app.
  • To confirm Premium purchases.
  • To keep SwimPeak safe: review reports, remove content, ban accounts that break our rules, limit abuse and spam, and apply usage limits.
  • To fix problems, protect our systems and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who can see your information

You can only see SwimPeak content when you are logged in, and so can everyone else. Nothing here is visible to people who are not logged in.

Public and Private accounts

  • Public account: any logged-in user can see your profile, your posts set to “Everyone”, and your stats, except for categories you have separately restricted.
  • Private account: any logged-in user who finds you can see your name, username, profile photo and your follower and following counts. They cannot see who your followers or following are, your posts, your stats or your personal bests unless they are your friend. People must send a follow request, which you approve.
  • Friends means two people who follow each other. For private accounts, the follow has to be approved.

Posts, categories and leaderboards

  • Each post has its own setting: “Everyone”, “Friends” or “Only me”. Private accounts can use “Friends” and “Only me”. If you switch an account to Private, posts you set to “Everyone” are treated as “Friends”. Anyone who can see a post can like and comment on it.
  • Categories: in Settings you can restrict individual categories (workout history, race results, personal bests, statistics, photos, achievements, followers list, following list) to Friends only or Private.
  • Leaderboards and search: Public accounts can appear on Global, National and State leaderboards with their name, username and stats. Private accounts are only shown to their friends. You can be found in search by your name or username (the start of it). We do not let anyone search by email address.

What other users never see

  • Your email address, your date of birth and your password.
  • Your AI Coach conversations, journal entries and nutrition logs, unless you choose to share something from them in a post.

Direct messages

Only you and the other person in a conversation can see its messages and images. Messages are stored by us and are not end-to-end encrypted, which means the people who run SwimPeak can technically access them. We do not read private messages, except to investigate a report, keep people safe, or meet a legal requirement. If two people are not friends, each can send only one message until the other replies.

Blocking and reporting

When you block someone, any follow or friend connection between you is removed, you stop seeing each other’s profiles and posts, and they cannot message you. When you report a user, post or comment, we store the report and the relevant content so we can review it. Our administrators can see reports, remove content and ban accounts.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only with the services we need to run SwimPeak, and only what they need:

  • Supabase, which provides our database, sign-in, file storage and server functions.
  • AI providers, who process the content you send to AI features in order to produce a response for you.
  • Google, for Google Play Billing and Google Fonts.
  • jsDelivr, which delivers code libraries used by the app.
  • GitHub, which hosts the website files for SwimPeak.
  • An email provider, to send account emails such as password resets.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect someone’s safety, or if SwimPeak is transferred to another owner (in which case this policy would continue to apply to your information).

5. Where your information is processed

Our providers may store and process your information in countries other than where you live. We choose providers that protect information with appropriate safeguards.

6. Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections, access rules in our database so users can only reach their own data or data shared with them, and private storage for photos and message images, which are delivered through short-lived links. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach affects you, we will notify you as required by law.

7. How long we keep your information, and deleting your account

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account yourself in the app (Settings, then Delete my account), or by emailing swimpeakteam@gmail.com. See our account deletion page for the steps.

When your account is deleted, we permanently delete your profile, posts, comments, likes, follows, blocks, notifications, training, nutrition and AI data, the messages you sent, and the photos and files you uploaded. The other person keeps their own side of any conversation, with you shown as “Swimmer” and no name or photo. Copies in backups may remain for a short time before they are overwritten. We may keep information we are legally required to keep. An account that is suspended or banned cannot be deleted while the suspension is in place.

8. Children and teenagers

You must be at least 13 to use SwimPeak, and if you are under 18 you need a parent or guardian’s permission. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn we have, we will delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and think your child has joined without permission, contact us and we will remove the account.

We collect date of birth to check age. Accounts created by people under 18 start as Private. You can change this in Settings, but we encourage younger swimmers to stay Private, to be careful about posting photos, and to only accept friends they know.

9. Your choices and rights

  • You can edit most of your profile in Settings, choose Public or Private, restrict individual categories, change a post’s visibility, turn notifications on or off, and block or report other users.
  • You can delete your account yourself in Settings. You can also ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, or delete your account and data by emailing swimpeakteam@gmail.com. We may need to confirm it is really you.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have extra rights, such as objecting to how we use your information or asking us to restrict it. Where laws like the GDPR apply, we rely on our contract with you (to run your account), our legitimate interests (keeping SwimPeak safe), and your consent where it is required, for example for health-related information you choose to enter.
  • If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first. You can also complain to your local privacy regulator. In Australia that is the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make an important change, we will tell you in the app. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
