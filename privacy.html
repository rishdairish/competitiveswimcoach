Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains what information SwimPeak collects and how it's used.

1. Information We Collect

Account info: name, username, email, date of birth, country, swimming club, bio, profile photo and banner image — provided when you sign up or edit your profile.
Training data: workouts, nutrition logs, competition results, personal bests, journal entries, and goals — entered by you.
Photos & videos: profile picture, banner image, photos attached to posts, and any meal photos, food-label photos, or swim-technique videos you upload for AI analysis.
AI Coach conversations: the questions and messages you send to the AI Coach chat.
Social activity: friends, follow relationships, posts, comments, likes, direct messages with other users, and any reports you submit about another user or a post.
Payment info: if you subscribe to Premium, payment is handled directly by Google Play Billing inside the Android app — we never see or store your full card details ourselves.

2. How We Use Your Information

To run the app's core features (tracking your training, showing your feed, enabling friends/messages), to generate AI-powered nutrition analysis, video technique analysis, journal feedback, race analysis, workout plans, and AI Coach responses based on the data and files you submit, to review reports and enforce our Terms, and to process Premium subscription payments through our payment provider.

3. Who Can See Your Information

Your account is either Public or Private (set in your Profile): a Private account limits your posts, profile details, and stats to accepted friends only, while a Public account makes them visible to anyone — except for individual categories (workout history, race results, personal bests, statistics, photos, achievements, followers list, following list) that you've separately restricted to Friends only or Private. Direct messages are only visible to you and the other person in the conversation.

4. AI Processing & Other Service Providers

Some features send your content to third-party AI/machine-learning service providers for processing on our behalf — for example, a meal photo or food-label photo you upload, a swim-technique video you submit for analysis, or a message you send to the AI Coach. These providers process that content to generate the analysis or response shown to you, under contractual terms that limit their use of it.

5. Data Storage

Your data is stored using Supabase, a third-party database/hosting provider, with security rules restricting access to your own data (or data you've chosen to share, like posts visible to friends).

6. Content Moderation

If you or someone else submits a report about a user or a post, the report and relevant content are stored so they can be reviewed. Blocking a user prevents them from messaging you or seeing your profile, and removes any existing friend connection between you.

7. Children's Privacy

This app may be used by minors (e.g. competitive youth swimmers) under a parent or guardian's guidance. We collect date of birth as part of standard sign-up. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate parental consent, please contact us so we can address it.

8. Your Choices

You can update most of your profile and account information at any time from your Settings page, turn notifications on or off, set your account to Public or Private and fine-tune individual sharing categories, and block or report other users. Full account and data deletion isn't yet self-service in the app — contact us to request it and we'll action it manually.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time; continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to access or delete your data, can be directed to the app's developer.

This is a general-purpose template, not legal advice — it should be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before relying on it for a real, publicly launched app, especially given the involvement of minors, AI processing, and payment processing.
